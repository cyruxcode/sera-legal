This Privacy Policy explains what information the iOS app "Sera: Plant Care" ("Sera", "the app") handles, why, and what choices you have. In this policy, "we" means the developer named above.

1. Summary

  • Sera has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or password.
  • We operate no server that receives your plant data. Your data stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. We cannot read it.
  • The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To do so, Google processes certain device data as described in section 5.

2. Information stored in the app

You enter the plants you keep (name, species, placement, pot details), your care records and your settings. The app also keeps your level and badge progress. This information is created by you and used only to run the app: schedules, reminders, the calendar and progress.

If iCloud is enabled on your device, this data is synced between your devices through your own private Apple CloudKit database. It is governed by your Apple Account and Apple's terms and is not accessible to us.

3. Camera

The light meter reads the front camera's exposure values to estimate how bright a spot is. The camera image is never displayed, recorded, stored or transmitted. The app asks for camera permission before first use; you can withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Notifications

Care reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent from a server and no notification data leaves your device.

5. Advertising

The free app shows a Google AdMob banner and, after you reach three plants, an optional rewarded ad that you may choose to watch to add an extra plant. You are never required to watch an ad.

  • What Google may process: to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, Google may process your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and other device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and app performance and crash data.
  • Your choices: where required by law, we ask for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the app works the same and you see non-personalized ads. You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Google as an independent controller: Google handles this data under its own terms. See the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We rely on your consent for advertising-related processing and tracking, which you can withdraw at any time without affecting the use of the app. Handling the data you enter in the app on your device is necessary to provide the app you asked for.

7. Retention and deletion

Your plant data is kept on your device until you delete it or remove the app. In Settings > My Data you can export everything as a JSON file or erase all data; erasing also removes it from your iCloud copy. Deleting the app removes the on-device data; manage any remaining iCloud copy in iOS Settings > Apple Account > iCloud.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of personal data, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests can be handled directly in the app (section 7). For anything else, contact us at the address below. Requests concerning data processed by Google should be directed to Google.

9. International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries outside your own. Google relies on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required.

10. Children

Sera is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under the higher age set by local law). If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Security

Data stays on your device and in your private iCloud storage and is protected by the security features of iOS and iCloud. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we do not collect your plant data on our own servers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for material changes, inform you in the app or in the App Store release notes.
